Number Ten

It still thinks the game isn't over. You're lost in a maze: a night jungle, endless yellow rooms, or the tunnels under the old gym. And it's hunting you.

How to get out

  1. Number Ten is blind, but it hears everything. Running is loud, walking is quieter, standing still is silent.
  2. Find the 3 spear shards hidden in the maze.
  3. With the spear whole, strike it 3 times up close. Each hit drives it back.
  4. Once it's dead, the exit door unlocks. Follow the light out.

You're not alone: Finn #150 got lost down here too. He'll follow you, tell you where to go and warn you when it's close.

Controls

  • Move: WASD or arrows. Look: mouse (click the view) or Q / E.
  • Run: Shift. Sneak: hold Ctrl or C. Strike: Space or F. Map: M. Flashlight on/off: L. Ask Finn for help: T.
  • Phone: left side moves (tap SNEAK, or push the stick only a little, to move quietly), drag the view to look, buttons for run, light, strike, talk and map. With your light off it's harder for Number Ten to spot you.
